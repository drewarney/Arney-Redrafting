About SLOTD

Drafting support for shops that would rather be cutting chips.

San Luis Obispo Technical Drafting is a contract drafting and design service run by mechanical engineering student Drew Arney, working with shops and hardware teams across the country.

We build 3D models and production drawings from whatever you have: a sketch, a worn-out part, a photo, or a print that's been faxed one too many times. You get clean, machinist-ready prints without tying up your programmers or engineers.

How it works

  1. Send what you haveA sketch, photo, sample part, old print, or STEP file.
  2. Get a fixed quoteA price and a delivery date, usually within one business day.
  3. Review a draftMark it up; revisions are handled before anything is final.
  4. Receive your filesNative CAD, STEP, and a PDF drawing set, ready for the floor.

What we offer

Services

From a single part to a full assembly, every drawing is checked for manufacturability before it leaves the desk.

Your CAD, your standards

Most work is done in Autodesk Fusion. If your team runs SolidWorks, Inventor, Creo, Onshape, NX, or another system, provide a license or seat and I'll work directly in your environment, using your templates, title blocks, and file conventions.
