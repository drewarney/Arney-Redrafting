Who We Are

A contract drafting and design service based in San Luis Obispo, California, run by mechanical engineering student Drew Arney and serving clients nationwide.

What We Do

We build 3D models and production drawings from whatever you have: a sketch, a worn-out part, a photo, or a print that's been faxed one too many times.

How It Works

Send what you have, get a fixed quote and a date, review a draft, and receive native files (Fusion or your CAD system), STEP, and a PDF drawing set.
