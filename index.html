About the company

About DAC

DAC is a contract CAD and drafting company based in San Luis Obispo, California, working with machine shops and hardware teams across the U.S.

We turn sketches, sample parts, old prints, and shop measurements into clean 3D models and machinist-ready drawings, with GD&T per ASME Y14.5 on every print. Every job starts with a fixed quote and a delivery date, and every drawing is checked for manufacturability before it leaves the desk.
